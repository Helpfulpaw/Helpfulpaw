# Alexey Pakhomov

**Wisdom as a Service Coalitions**

I’m Alexey Pakhomov. I’m building an open coalition of people and agents. I want you to be able to copy how it works, change what you need and build your own.

Skills should belong to people. Don’t pay for knowledge that’s already open. Pay for human care and attention.

My three goals: more accurate results at lower inference cost; people in control of their attention, context and actions; and users influencing product quality through organized, professional feedback to companies.

Want to contribute? Bring one real task, the result you expected and what actually happened. A fix, a reusable skill or a useful bug report all help. Give companies feedback they can act on.

Start for free: Personal OS and attention skills for people; AI Supervision for agents choosing tools. Out of work? Your first consultation is free: your own website and ways to turn your professional experience into skills for AI agents.

If this speaks to you, follow and use what I’m building. If it helps, share it, improve it and support humanity’s open CAPEX.
